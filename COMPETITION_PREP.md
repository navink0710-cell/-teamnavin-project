# Competition Preparation

A general preparation workflow:

### Phase 1 — Base
Build work capacity, technical consistency and foundational strength.

### Phase 2 — Strength
Increase specificity and develop heavier competition-relevant work.

### Phase 3 — Specificity
Practice commands, competition standards, attempt selection and platform-specific execution.

### Phase 4 — Taper
Reduce fatigue while retaining useful intensity and technical exposure.

### Competition week
Confirm:
- Weight-class strategy
- Attempts
- Equipment
- Commands
- Travel
- Food and hydration plan
- Warm-up sequence
- Emergency contingencies
